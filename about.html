About Me.

My Journey

I've completed my BS in Software Engineering at the University of Management and Technology in Lahore. Most of what I build sits on the MERN stack: React for the interface, Node.js and Express for REST APIs, and MongoDB for data. When a project needs a relational model I reach for PostgreSQL and plain SQL.

On the front end I care about solid JavaScript fundamentals, components that are easy to reuse, and layouts that still work on a small phone. On the back end I focus on clear API routes, authentication and integrating third-party services such as Stripe.

My MERN Stack internship at U Devs SMC Pvt Ltd showed me how features move from idea to release inside a team, using Git-based workflows. I learn by building, and I'm always working on the next thing I don't yet know how to do.
